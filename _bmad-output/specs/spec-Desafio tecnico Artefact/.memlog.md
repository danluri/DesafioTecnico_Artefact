- (capability) CAP-10: Optional WhatsApp, market comparison, and MCP adapters can reuse P0 use cases without becoming P0 dependencies.
- (constraint) Operational facts come from the supplied CSV snapshot; policy facts come from the supplied policy source with provenance.
- (constraint) Order access requires a server-established Authorized Principal; conversation text cannot authenticate a Customer.
- (constraint) The offline P0 path requires no external credentials and must not expose raw customer PII by default.
- (constraint) Business rules remain deterministic and outside the language model; optional integrations cannot change official Product price.
- (constraint) The implementation follows the finalized hexagonal architecture spine and its dependency, ownership, tool, and observability rules.
- (constraint) Customer-facing responses use Brazilian Portuguese and disclose uncertainty, snapshot freshness, and unavailable capabilities.

- (assumption) An explicitly labeled simulated Authorized Principal is acceptable for local demonstrations.
- (assumption) Raw customer CSV and internal policy PDF will not be published without permission; anonymized fixtures may be used.
